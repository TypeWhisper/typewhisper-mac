TypeWhisperEar.mlmodelc ("TypeWhisper Ear") is a spoken language identification model built by
TypeWhisper. It contains the encoder and decoder weights of OpenAI's whisper-tiny
(https://huggingface.co/openai/whisper-tiny, Apache License 2.0), unchanged except for 8-bit weight
palettization, plus a classifier trained by TypeWhisper.

The classifier was trained on:
- FLEURS by Google (https://huggingface.co/datasets/google/fleurs), CC BY 4.0
- VoxLingua107 by Jörgen Valk and Tanel Alumäe, Tallinn University of Technology
  (https://cs.taltech.ee/staff/tanel.alumae/data/voxlingua107/), CC BY 4.0
- VoxPopuli by Meta (https://github.com/facebookresearch/voxpopuli), CC0 1.0
- AMI Meeting Corpus (https://groups.inf.ed.ac.uk/ami/corpus/), CC BY 4.0
None of these recordings are included.
