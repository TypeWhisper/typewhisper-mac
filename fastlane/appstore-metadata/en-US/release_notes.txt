TypeWhisper 1.8.2 makes ElevenLabs dictations much faster, uploads less audio for the live preview with cloud engines and lets you import your own Parakeet models.

• ElevenLabs: short dictations no longer wait about 15 seconds after you stop speaking. The new Scribe v2 Medical model and more languages are available, and German and other languages are formatted correctly with automatic detection.
• Live preview: cloud engines that cannot stream, such as Groq or ElevenLabs, no longer upload the last seconds of audio again every few seconds. The preview runs on a local engine instead, while the final text still comes from your dictation engine.
• Parakeet: import your own Parakeet models converted to Core ML, for example a fine-tune for one language.
• Apple Speech: pick Hong Kong Cantonese with Traditional Chinese characters. Language Detection tells Cantonese apart from Mandarin.
• Dictation into Flutter apps and the kitty terminal works reliably.
• The HTTP API can return the time of each word.
• The "New in TypeWhisper" window stays in front of Settings and has a single Continue button.
