Private speech-to-text for your Mac.

TypeWhisper turns your voice into text in any Mac app. Press a shortcut, speak, and the text appears where you are typing. Transcribe with on-device AI models or with optional cloud providers, and turn the result into finished text with your own workflows.

DICTATE EVERYWHERE

• Start dictation with a global shortcut, as push-to-talk, toggle, or both
• Insert text into the frontmost app, or copy it to the clipboard
• See your speech while you talk with Live Transcript
• Paste your last dictation again or pick one of your recent ones

ON-DEVICE OR CLOUD

• Transcribe on your Mac with WhisperKit, Parakeet, Qwen3 ASR, or Apple Speech
• Optionally use cloud engines such as OpenAI, Groq, Deepgram, ElevenLabs, AssemblyAI, Gemini, Soniox, or Speechmatics with your own API key
• Choose engines, languages, and models per workflow

WORKFLOWS AND AI WRITING

• Clean up, rewrite, summarize, or translate dictated text before it is inserted
• Run workflows automatically in specific apps
• Use Apple Intelligence, a local language model, or providers like Claude, OpenAI, Gemini, and OpenRouter
• Rewrite selected text from the prompt palette
• Translate on your Mac with Apple Translate

YOUR WORDS, YOUR WAY

• Teach TypeWhisper names and specialist terms with a personal dictionary
• Expand snippets with date, time, and clipboard placeholders
• Remove filler words automatically
• Search your history and see your dictation statistics

FILES AND INTEGRATIONS

• Transcribe audio and video files, also from Finder with "Transcribe with TypeWhisper"
• Send results to Obsidian, Linear, or webhooks, and connect MCP servers

TYPEWHISPER PREMIUM

• Meeting automation: get a reminder before calendar meetings or start recording automatically when you join
• Speaker detection: see who said what in recordings and transcribed files, detected on your Mac
• Cloud sync: keep your dictionary and snippets in sync across your devices through iCloud or a folder you choose
• iPhone and iPad: dictate and write on your iPhone or iPad with this Mac's models and workflows

Premium is a universal purchase: buy it once on Mac, iPhone, or iPad and use it on all of them with the same Apple Account.

PRIVACY FIRST

• On-device models keep your audio on your Mac
• Cloud providers are optional and only used when you set them up
• No advertising or third-party tracking

Dictation, on-device models, workflows, the dictionary, and snippets work without an account or subscription. Some models need Apple silicon, a model download, or an internet connection. Cloud providers require your own credentials. Inserting text into other apps uses the optional Accessibility permission; without it, TypeWhisper copies the text to the clipboard.
