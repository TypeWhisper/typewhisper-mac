TypeWhisper 1.8.3 transcribes with more cloud engines while you speak, saves every Recorder transcript to History and no longer cuts off the last word with push-to-talk.

• Live dictation: AssemblyAI, Mistral AI, Reson8 and ElevenLabs transcribe while you speak. The live preview and the Live Transcript window show the provider's own transcript, and the final text is ready a fraction of a second after you stop.
• Push-to-talk keeps recording up to 350 ms after you release the key while you are still speaking, so the last word is no longer lost.
• Recorder: every transcribed recording gets a History record with its transcript, name, duration and engine, so search, sync and exports include it. With calls on speakers, the other participants are no longer credited to "Me".
• Dictations no longer ask cloud engines for speaker labels, which providers bill on top of the transcription.
• Premium: redeem App Store offer codes on the Premium page (macOS 15 and later).
• New models and settings for AssemblyAI, Mistral AI, Reson8 and Microsoft AI. Soniox uses its new STT v6.
• Recordings recover after undocking a Mac from clamshell mode, other apps' audio is no longer turned down during calls on a MacBook, and the interface follows the language set for TypeWhisper in System Settings.
